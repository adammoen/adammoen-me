# 30-Day Immediate Backlink Action Plan

## Week 1: Foundation & Quick Wins

### Day 1: HARO Setup & Resource Research
**Time: 2 hours**
- [ ] Sign up for HARO (3x daily emails)
- [ ] Set up Google Alerts for:
  - "mental health expert" + "quote"
  - "workplace wellness" + "expert"
  - "keynote speaker" + "mental health"
  - "family organization" + "expert"
- [ ] Create Twitter list of journalists who cover your topics
- [ ] Install Check My Links browser extension

### Day 2-3: Resource Page Research
**Time: 3 hours total**
- [ ] Google search: "mental health resources for employers" + "links"
- [ ] Google search: "workplace wellness speakers" + "directory" 
- [ ] Google search: "family organization tools" + "resources"
- [ ] Create spreadsheet of 50 target resource pages
- [ ] Prioritize by domain authority (use MozBar extension)

### Day 4-5: First HARO Responses
**Time: 30 min daily**
- [ ] Respond to 1-2 relevant HARO queries daily
- [ ] Use templates but personalize each response
- [ ] Track responses in spreadsheet
- [ ] Set up Google Alerts for your name (monitor mentions)

### Day 6-7: Resource Page Outreach
**Time: 2 hours**
- [ ] Send 10 personalized resource page emails
- [ ] Use templates but customize each one
- [ ] Focus on sites with DA 30+ first
- [ ] Schedule follow-ups for 2 weeks

## Week 2: Scaling Outreach

## High-Probability Quick Wins (First 7 Days)

### Target These First (90%+ Success Rate):
1. **Local Business Journals** - They need expert sources
2. **Industry Association Newsletters** - Always need content
3. **Podcast Appearances** - Most hosts say yes to good pitches
4. **HARO Responses** - If you're fast and specific
5. **Resource Pages** - High success rate with personalization

## Red Flags to Avoid:

❌ **Don't Do:**
- Mass email identical templates
- Buy links from link farms
- Use automated outreach tools initially
- Ignore relationship building for quick links
- Forget to follow up (80% of success comes from follow-ups)

✅ **Do This Instead:**
- Personalize every outreach email
- Build genuine relationships first
- Provide value before asking for links
- Follow up professionally and persistently
- Track everything for optimization

## Month 2 Preview:
Once you've mastered the basics in month 1, month 2 focuses on:
- Scaling successful strategies
- Creating more linkable assets
- Building media relationships
- Guest posting on tier 1 publications
- Launching link-worthy campaigns (surveys, studies)

The key is starting with high-probability wins to build momentum and learn what works for your specific niche and personality. Then scale the successful approaches while testing new strategies.
