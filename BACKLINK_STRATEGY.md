## Strategic Backlink Acquisition Methods

### 1. Expert Roundups & Quote Contributions
**High Success Rate: 70-80%**

**Process:**
1. Search Google for: "mental health experts" + "roundup" + "2025"
2. Look for journalists asking for expert quotes on Twitter/LinkedIn
3. Use HARO (Help a Reporter Out) for media requests
4. Monitor Brand24/Mention for "mental health speaker" queries

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: Mental Health Speaking Expert - 500+ Corporate Keynotes

Hi [Name],

I saw your roundup on [topic] and wanted to contribute. As someone who's delivered 500+ mental health keynotes to Fortune 500 companies, I have specific insights on [relevant angle].

Here's my take: [2-3 sentence expert opinion with data/example]

Bio: Adam Moen is a certified Mental Health First Aid instructor and creator of AvaLoChat (50K+ users). His keynotes have been featured in Harvard Business Review.

Link: https://adammoen.me/blog/[relevant-article]

Best,
Adam
```

### 2. Resource Page Link Building
**Success Rate: 40-50% with personalization**

**Target Searches:**
- "mental health resources for employers"
- "workplace wellness speakers"
- "family organization tools"
- "children's nutrition resources"

**Outreach Template:**
```
Subject: Addition to Your [Page Title] Resource List

Hi [Name],

I found your [resource page name] while researching [topic]. It's incredibly comprehensive!

I noticed you link to [mention 2-3 existing resources]. I've created a detailed guide on [your topic] that your audience might find valuable: [link]

It includes [specific unique value - data, framework, case studies].

Would this be a good fit for your resource list?

Best,
Adam Moen
[credentials]
```

### 3. Broken Link Building
**Success Rate: 30-40% but highly targeted**

**Tools Needed:**
- Ahrefs or SEMrush for broken link analysis
- Check My Links browser extension

**Process:**
1. Find relevant sites with broken links to mental health/speaking content
2. Create similar or better content
3. Reach out suggesting your content as replacement
