## Natural Writing Style Guidelines

### Conversational Elements
- Start articles with reader questions: "Have you ever wondered why..."
- Use rhetorical questions throughout: "So what does this mean for you?"
- Include personal anecdotes: "I remember when I first..."
- Address reader directly: "You might be thinking..."

### Sentence Variation
- **Short impact:** "It works."
- **Medium explanation:** "This approach combines vulnerability with professional boundaries."  
- **Longer storytelling:** "When I delivered my first healthcare keynote, I watched as 200 nurses shifted from skeptical crossed arms to leaning forward, asking questions about their own wellness—that's when I knew authentic sharing could transform professional environments."

### Remove AI-isms
- Avoid: "In conclusion," "Furthermore," "It's important to note"
- Replace with: "Here's what I've learned," "Plus," "I've found that"
- Eliminate generic phrases: "comprehensive guide," "best practices," "cutting-edge"
