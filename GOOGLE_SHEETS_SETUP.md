# Google Sheets Tracking System Setup

## Sheet 1: HARO Tracking

### Column Headers (Row 1):
A: Date
B: Query Topic  
C: Publication/Journalist
D: My Response Summary
E: Status
F: Follow-up Date
G: Result
H: Notes

### Example Entries:
```
Date | Query Topic | Publication | Response Summary | Status | Follow-up | Result | Notes
12/1 | Workplace Burnout | Forbes | Shared 3 signs from keynotes | Sent | 12/3 | Quoted in article | Great exposure
12/1 | Mental Health Apps | TechCrunch | AvaLoChat human vs AI angle | Sent | 12/3 | No response | Try again later
```

### Status Options:
- Sent
- Responded - Quoted
- Responded - Not Used
- No Response
- Following Up

## Sheet 4: Results Summary (Weekly Updates)

### Column Headers:
A: Week of
B: HARO Responses Sent
C: HARO Links Gained
D: Resource Emails Sent
E: Resource Links Gained
F: Podcast Pitches Sent
G: Podcasts Booked
H: Total New Links
I: Best Win This Week

### Weekly Goals:
Week 1: 5 HARO, 15 Resource emails, 1-2 links
Week 2: 7 HARO, 20 Resource emails, 3 Podcast pitches, 3-5 links
Week 3: 10 HARO, 25 Resource emails, 5 Podcast pitches, 5-8 links
Week 4: 10 HARO, 20 Resource emails, 10 Podcast pitches, 8-12 links

## Setup Instructions:

1. Go to sheets.google.com
2. Create new spreadsheet: "Adam Moen - Backlink Tracking"
3. Create 4 tabs with names above
4. Copy column headers into each tab
5. Set up conditional formatting:
   - Green = Success/Links Gained
   - Yellow = Pending/Follow-up Needed  
   - Red = Rejected/No Response
6. Share with yourself and bookmark for daily access
